Top 10 Most Popular FAQ`s

For first-time pet owners, there are things that you may not know for. Here are some things that might help you from our professionals.

What should I feed my new pet?

Choose a complete and balanced food appropriate for your pet’s age, size, and breed. Puppies and kittens need higher protein and calories, while adults need maintenance diets. If unsure, ask us—our staff can recommend the best food for your pet’s needs.

How do I know if my pet is eating enough?

Monitor your pet situation, if they have consistent appetite, have an steady, healthy weight gain (for young pets) and normal stool consistency. However, If your pet is losing weight or refusing food, consult a vet.

How can I bathe my pet and how often?

Dogs: once every 2–4 weeks depending on breed and activity. Cats: usually do not need frequent baths unless recommended by a vet. Use pet-safe shampoo only.

When should I take my pet to the vet?

Book a first vet visit within the first week. Puppies and kittens need vaccination schedules, deworming, and health checks. Adult pets should visit the vet once or twice a year.

Food dogs should not eat:

Chocolate - toxic to the heart and nervous system. Grapes & Raisins – can cause kidney failure. Onions & Garlic – damage red blood cells. Avocado – contains persin, harmful to dogs. Alcohol – extremely toxic. Coffee, Tea & Caffeine – dangerous stimulant.

How do I know my pet is healthy?

Healthy signs include: Bright eyes, shiny coat, good appetite, normal poop, active and alert behavior. However if sudden changes such as vomiting, no appetite, diarrhea, please require vet attention immedietly.

How do I choose the right treat?

Select treats that match your pet’s size, age, and dietary needs. Avoid giving too many—treats should make up no more than 10% of daily calories. We can help match treats to your pet’s diet.

Which supplements does my pet need?

Not all pets need supplements, but some benefit from, probiotics (digestion), omega oils (skin & coat), joint support (for large breeds or seniors) Ask our team—we’ll suggest safe and suitable options.

What supplies do I need as a new pet owner?

Essentials include: food, water bowl, collar/harness, leash, bed, litter box (for cats), grooming tools, toys, and ID tag. Your starter kit can be simple, and you can add more as your pet settles in.

How do I start training my pet?

Begin with simple commands like sit, stay, and come. Use positive reinforcement—treats, praise, or play. Short, fun training sessions every day help build trust and good habits.
